# Proposal

## Why

The 1:1 and feedback templates exist. Nothing says how an EM works with ICs, how EMs work with each other, or how a Senior EM works with EMs and above.

## What Changes

- Add `public/organisation/management-interfaces.md` with those three interfaces.
- Each interface points at the 1:1 template and the feedback template. It does not copy them.
- Role names match `management-ladder`. Do not add a PM or Design interface here.
- Link it from the readme under Organisation, as "Management interfaces".

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `management-interfaces`: How EM, IC, and Senior EM roles work with each other.

### Modified Capabilities

None. `openspec/specs/` is empty.

## Impact

Files added: `public/organisation/management-interfaces.md`. Files touched: `readme.md`.

Depends on `management-ladder` for EM3, Senior EM, and Director.

Links: `1_1.md` and `feedback.md` stay the conversation templates. PM and Design approval stays in the discovery note and in `product-framework`.
